
# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in our community a welcoming and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards
Be respectful, welcoming, and constructive. Avoid harassment, discrimination, insults, and personal attacks.

## Enforcement Responsibilities
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior.

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces and when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement
Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to project maintainers through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially where possible.

## Enforcement Guidelines
Maintainers may use correction, warning, temporary ban, or permanent ban depending on the severity and repetition of misconduct.
